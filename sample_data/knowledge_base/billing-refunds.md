# Billing and Refund Policy

## Seat-Based Billing

CloudDesk charges per active seat per month. A "seat" is any user who has logged in at least once during the billing period.

### Plan Pricing (Monthly)

| Plan | Price/Seat | Min Seats | Storage | SSO | API Access |
|------|------------|-----------|---------|-----|------------|
| Free | $0 | 1 | 10 GB | No | Limited |
| Pro | $15 | 3 | 100 GB | No | Full |
| Business | $35 | 10 | 1 TB | Yes | Full |
| Enterprise | $65 | 25 | Unlimited | Yes + SCIM | Full + SLA |

## Proration Rules

- **Adding seats**: Prorated from activation date to end of billing period
- **Removing seats**: No credit for current period; takes effect next billing cycle
- **Plan upgrades**: Immediate, prorated charge for difference
- **Plan downgrades**: Effective next billing cycle; no refund for current period

## Refund Eligibility

### Eligible for Refund
- Billing errors (e.g., charged for deactivated seats that were removed before period start)
- Service outages exceeding SLA (Enterprise only)
- Duplicate charges

### Not Eligible for Refund
- Unused seats during billing period
- Voluntary plan downgrades mid-cycle
- Feature requests not delivered
- Customer-initiated account closure

## Refund Process

1. **Submit request** via support with details
2. **Verification** — Support confirms billing error
3. **Approval** — Refunds > $0 require agent approval (policy)
4. **Processing** — 5-10 business days to original payment method

## Common Scenarios

### "Charged for seats we deactivated"
If seats were deactivated **before** the billing period started, you'll receive a prorated credit. If deactivated **during** the period, no credit for that period.

### "Upgraded by mistake"
Contact support within 48 hours for reversal. After 48 hours, the upgrade stands for the current period.

### "Enterprise SLA breach"
Enterprise customers with documented downtime exceeding 99.9% uptime SLA receive service credits per contract.
